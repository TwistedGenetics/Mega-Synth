MEGA SYNTH RACK (test skin) - installing on a Mac
It installs beside the normal Mega Synth; both can be used at once.
================================

This folder contains:
  Mega Synth Rack.vst3        VST3 plugin (Ableton Live, Bitwig, Reaper, Studio One, FL Studio, Cubase...)
  Mega Synth Rack.component   Audio Unit plugin (Logic Pro, GarageBand, also Live/Reaper)
  Mega Synth Rack.app         Standalone app (plays from a MIDI keyboard or your computer keyboard)

1. Copy the plugins into place

   Open Finder, press Shift+Cmd+G and paste:   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   Drag "Mega Synth Rack.vst3" into that folder.

   Press Shift+Cmd+G again and paste:          ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
   Drag "Mega Synth Rack.component" into that folder.

   (Create either folder if it doesn't exist.)

2. Let macOS run it

   The plugin isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate, so macOS
   blocks it the first time ("damaged" or "can't be verified"). Open Terminal
   and paste these lines, then press Return:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Mega Synth Rack.vst3"
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Mega Synth Rack.component"

   For the standalone app, after moving it to Applications:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/"Mega Synth Rack.app"

3. Rescan in your DAW

   Restart your DAW (or use its plugin rescan). Mega Synth shows up as an
   instrument under "Twisted Genetics". In Logic, if it doesn't appear, open
   Logic > Settings > Plug-in Manager and click "Reset & Rescan Selection".

Bringing patches across from the browser version
   In the browser synth press "Save Patch" (copies the patch to the clipboard),
   then in the plugin press "Paste Patch". It works the other way too: "Copy
   Patch" in the plugin, then "Load Patch" in the browser.
